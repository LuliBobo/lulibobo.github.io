>
> By the time the report is ready, the moment to act is gone.
>
> An AI agent watches the numbers in real time and flags the gap the day it appears — not at month-end.
>
> What's the oldest data your last big decision was based on?

**Vizuál:** žiadny (čistý text hook funguje), alebo jednoduchý „Month-end report (too late) vs Real-time flag (in time)" timeline.

## PIATOK — Formát A (Porovnanie / 1 diagram)

**Post:**

> CFO vs Controller vs FP&A — who actually does what?
>
> Controller: makes sure the numbers are right (the past).
> FP&A: explains what they mean and what's next (the future).
> CFO: decides what to do about it (the call).
>
> Most people confuse all three. AI is now touching all of them.

**Vizuál (povinný):** porovnávacia grafika 3 stĺpce — Controller / FP&A / CFO, každý s 1 vetou. Vysoko zdieľateľné, ľudia sa identifikujú s rolami.
**CTA:** „Which one are you?" prípadne pridaj do prvej odpovede.

## Checklist pred publikovaním (každý post)

- [ ] Žiadne hashtagy

- [ ] Vizuál pripravený, kde je označený ako povinný
- [ ] Po publikovaní: napíš 3–5 odpovedí pod väčšie účty (outbound)
- [ ] Odpíš každému, kto skomentuje
